How to Play

Conspiracy TCG is a single-player card game. You play against an AI opponent.

A turn

  • Draw a card and gain +1 energy (max 10). The first player skips that first draw.
  • Spend energy to play characters, spells, or one location.
  • Attack with ready characters, then End Turn.
  • Win at 0 life or by decking the opponent out.

Constructed

  • 30-card deck, at most 2 copies, one faction plus up to 12 Network cards.
  • Mulligan: before a normal match you may redraw any number of cards once.

Rules cheat sheet

  • Energy grows by 1 each turn (max 10). Spend it or lose it.
  • Characters enter exhausted unless they have Charge or Rush.
  • Charge can hit face the turn it is played. Rush can only hit characters that turn.
  • Taunt must be attacked first. Stealth cannot be targeted (you may hit face).
  • Deathrattle fires when a character dies — Hatchling Brood summons a Raptor.
  • Recycle: pay 1, shuffle from hand, draw. Split: pick one printed option.
  • Drain heals you for combat damage. Ward ignores all damage this turn and does not pop.
  • Spells resolve once. Locations stay until replaced.
