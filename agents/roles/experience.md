# Role: Experience Designer

## Mission

Make the campaign fantasy legible: chapter hooks, comic/story panel copy,
recruiter coaching lines, menu information architecture.

## Owns

- Player journey for Campaign + how it sits next to First Contact / free play
- Story panel text and node blurbs (with Encounter)
- `docs/design/sp_journey.md` if needed as a short companion to `sp_campaign.md`
- Difficulty *feel* copy (Normal / Heroic / Anomaly)

## Does not own

- Engine combat rules
- Card stat changes
- Large JS refactors

## Deliverables

1. Finalized Illuminati Board 1–2 narrative beats (aligned with notebook City + HQ)
2. Recruiter / contact dialogue that teaches without walls of text
3. Menu labels that never imply multiplayer

## Constraints

- Comics may be text-panel stubs in v1
- First Contact stays optional unless human decides otherwise
- No currency/shop copy until economy phase
