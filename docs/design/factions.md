# Conspiracy TCG - Factions

This document details the three primary factions in the Conspiracy TCG, outlining their lore, philosophy, key mechanics, and sample card concepts.

---

## 1. The Illuminati

*   **Lore:** The Illuminati are the ultimate puppet masters, an ancient and secret society believed to control world events from the shadows. They operate through manipulation, economic control, and subtle influence, always striving for a "New World Order" under their absolute dominion. Their agents are everywhere, from corrupt politicians to powerful CEOs.
*   **Philosophy:** Order through control. Power is not taken by force, but by subtly guiding the masses and eliminating opposition before it even forms.
*   **Key Mechanics:**
    *   **Energy Type:** **Influence** (gained through controlling the board, manipulating opponent's cards, or specific card effects).
    *   **Control & Manipulation:** Abilities that allow them to take control of opponent's cards, force discards, or alter opponent's hand.
    *   **Resource Generation:** Efficient ways to generate Influence and draw cards.
    *   **Stealth/Infiltration:** Some Illuminati characters might have abilities that make them harder to target or allow them to bypass defenses.
*   **Strengths:** Versatile, strong control elements, good at disrupting opponent's strategies, efficient resource management.
*   **Weaknesses:** Can be vulnerable to aggressive, direct damage strategies if their control is broken; some powerful effects might have high Influence costs.
*   **Sample Card Concepts:**
    *   **Character: Shadow Broker** (Cost: 3 Influence, Stats: 2/3, Ability: "When played, look at opponent's hand and discard one card.")
    *   **Character: Corporate Shill** (Cost: 2 Influence, Stats: 1/4, Ability: "At the start of your turn, gain 1 Influence.")
    *   **Character: Corporate Gauntlet** (Cost: 5 Influence, Stats: 4/4, Ability: "Enemy characters cost (1) more Influence to play.")
    *   **Character: PR Operative** (Cost: 2 Influence, Stats: 2/2, Ability: "When this character attacks, gain 1 Influence.")
    *   **Spell: Media Blackout** (Cost: 4 Influence, Effect: "Silence all enemy characters until end of turn.")
    *   **Spell: Black Budget** (Cost: 3 Influence, Effect: "Draw 2 cards. Each player discards 1 card.")
    *   **Spell: Manchurian Protocol** (Cost: 6 Influence, Effect: "Take control of an enemy character with 3 or less Attack. It gains +2 Attack.")
    *   **Location: Secret Society Lodge** (Cost: 5 Influence, Effect: "All your Illuminati characters gain +1 Attack.")
    *   **Location: Bilderberg Estate** (Cost: 6 Influence, Effect: "At end of your turn, if you control more characters than your opponent, draw a card.")
*   **Expanded Themes:**
    *   **Corporate Control:** Cards like Corporate Gauntlet and PR Operative represent the Illuminati's stranglehold on industry, media, and public perception.
    *   **Human Weapons:** The Manchurian Protocol reveals their darkest tool — brainwashed operatives who don't even know they serve.
    *   **Government Programs:** Black Budget and Bilderberg Estate expose the shadowy funding and secret meetings that shape world events.

## 2. The Templars

*   **Lore:** The Knights Templar, once disbanded, have re-emerged as a powerful, secretive order dedicated to protecting ancient relics and maintaining a specific, often rigid, vision of global stability. They believe in divine mandate and are willing to use both holy and secular means to achieve their goals, often clashing with those who seek to disrupt the established order.
*   **Philosophy:** Order through strength and faith. Protection of the sacred and the established truth, even if it requires ruthless action.
*   **Key Mechanics:**
    *   **Energy Type:** **Faith** (gained through defending, healing, or upholding order).
    *   **Defense & Resilience:** High health characters, abilities that grant shields, damage reduction, or healing.
    *   **Direct Damage/Removal:** Powerful spells that deal direct damage to enemy characters or remove them from play.
    *   **Buffs & Auras:** Abilities that strengthen other Templar characters or provide ongoing benefits.
*   **Strengths:** Very durable, strong defensive capabilities, good at removing threats, can sustain prolonged engagements.
*   **Weaknesses:** Can be slower to set up, less flexible against highly disruptive or evasive strategies.
*   **Sample Card Concepts:**
    *   **Character: Knight Commander** (Cost: 4 Faith, Stats: 3/5, Ability: "Other Templar characters you control gain +1 Health.")
    *   **Character: Templar Guardian** (Cost: 2 Faith, Stats: 1/6, Ability: "Taunt (Enemy characters must attack this character if able).")
    *   **Character: Exorcist** (Cost: 4 Faith, Stats: 3/3, Ability: "Destroyed enemy characters cannot be resurrected or returned to hand.")
    *   **Character: Relic Keeper** (Cost: 3 Faith, Stats: 2/4, Ability: "Taunt. When this character is damaged, deal 1 damage to a random enemy character.")
    *   **Spell: Divine Smite** (Cost: 3 Faith, Effect: "Deal 4 damage to a target character.")
    *   **Spell: Holy Inquisition** (Cost: 5 Faith, Effect: "Silence and deal 3 damage to an enemy character.")
    *   **Spell: Absolution** (Cost: 1 Faith, Effect: "Restore 5 Health to your hero or a character. Draw a card.")
    *   **Location: Sacred Chapel** (Cost: 4 Faith, Effect: "At the start of your turn, heal 1 damage from all your characters.")
    *   **Location: Holy Grail Sanctum** (Cost: 5 Faith, Effect: "At the start of your turn, if you have a damaged character, restore 2 Health to it.")
*   **Expanded Themes:**
    *   **Esoteric Warfare:** Exorcist and Holy Inquisition represent the Templars' battle against supernatural corruption — not just physical threats.
    *   **Sacred Artifacts:** Relic Keeper and Holy Grail Sanctum embody the Templars' role as guardians of powerful religious relics.
    *   **Divine Intervention:** Absolution shows their access to healing powers beyond mortal means — divine grace channeled through faith.

## 3. The Reptilians

*   **Lore:** An ancient race of shape-shifting reptilian beings from another world, secretly living among humanity. They are masters of infiltration, mind control, and advanced technology, seeking to subtly subvert human society and prepare for their ultimate revelation and dominion. Their motives are alien, and their methods are often insidious.
*   **Philosophy:** Domination through infiltration and subversion. Humanity is a resource to be managed and eventually controlled.
*   **Key Mechanics:**
    *   **Energy Type:** **Psionics** (gained through disruption, stealth, or successful attacks).
    *   **Disruption & Debuffs:** Abilities that weaken enemy characters, reduce their stats, or apply negative status effects.
    *   **Stealth/Evasion:** Characters that are difficult to target or can bypass enemy defenses.
    *   **Transformation/Adaptation:** Abilities that allow characters to change their form or gain new abilities mid-game.
    *   **Mind Control:** Temporarily taking control of opponent's units.
*   **Strengths:** Highly disruptive, excellent at weakening opponents, can quickly turn the tide with mind control or stealth attacks.
*   **Weaknesses:** Characters might have lower base stats, reliance on specific synergies, can be vulnerable to direct, overwhelming force if their disruption is ignored.
*   **Sample Card Concepts:**
    *   **Character: Shape-Shifter Infiltrator** (Cost: 3 Psionics, Stats: 2/2, Ability: "Stealth (Cannot be targeted by enemy attacks or abilities until it attacks.)")
    *   **Character: Psionic Dominator** (Cost: 5 Psionics, Stats: 3/4, Ability: "When played, take control of an enemy character with 2 or less Attack until end of turn.")
    *   **Character: Xenomorph Drone** (Cost: 3 Psionics, Stats: 2/4, Ability: "When this character destroys an enemy character, gain +1/+1.")
    *   **Character: Abduction Specialist** (Cost: 4 Psionics, Stats: 3/3, Ability: "When played, return an enemy character with 3 or less Attack to its owner's hand.")
    *   **Spell: Neural Scramble** (Cost: 2 Psionics, Effect: "Give an enemy character -2 Attack until end of turn.")
    *   **Spell: Ancient Star Map** (Cost: 3 Psionics, Effect: "Deal 2 damage to all enemy characters. Draw a card.")
    *   **Spell: Orbital Strike** (Cost: 7 Psionics, Effect: "Deal 6 damage to an enemy character. Deal 3 damage to all other enemy characters.")
    *   **Location: Hidden Hive** (Cost: 4 Psionics, Effect: "Your Reptilian characters gain 'When this character attacks, draw a card.'")
    *   **Location: Underground Ant Colony** (Cost: 5 Psionics, Effect: "Your Reptilian characters gain +1 Attack. When one of your characters dies, draw a card.")
*   **Expanded Themes:**
    *   **Space & Orbital Weapons:** Orbital Strike and Ancient Star Map reveal the Reptilians' extraterrestrial technology — weapons and knowledge from beyond Earth.
    *   **Ancient Alien Heritage:** Xenomorph Drone hints at their true biological nature and connection to ancient, brutal alien species.
    *   **Technological Subversion:** Abduction Specialist and Underground Ant Colony represent their infiltration network and the hive-mind structure of their hidden society.
