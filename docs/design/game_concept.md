# Project: Conspiracy TCG - Game Concept

## High-Level Overview

"Conspiracy TCG" is a strategic card game where players command factions vying for global dominance through manipulation, ancient power, and otherworldly influence. The game centers around powerful Character cards that form the core of a player's strategy, supported by Spells and Locations that enhance their abilities and control the battlefield.

## Theme

The game draws inspiration from popular conspiracy theories, reimagining them as powerful, warring factions. This provides a rich lore base and allows for creative card designs, abilities, and strategic depth. The tone is a blend of intrigue, ancient mystery, and sci-fi elements.

## Unique Selling Points

*   **Faction-Specific Energy System:** Each faction utilizes a unique type of energy (Influence, Faith, Psionics) to fuel their cards and abilities, leading to distinct playstyles and strategic considerations.
*   **Character-Centric Gameplay:** The primary focus is on deploying and enhancing powerful Character cards, making them the stars of the show.
*   **Dynamic Card Interactions:** Inspired by games like Hearthstone, the game aims for intuitive card interactions, where playing cards can visibly update stats and trigger combinations.
*   **Rich Lore & Thematic Depth:** The conspiracy theory theme offers endless possibilities for card names, abilities, and world-building.

## Core Gameplay Loop

Players take turns drawing cards, generating their faction's unique energy, and using that energy to:
1.  **Deploy Characters:** Bring powerful Character cards onto the field.
2.  **Cast Spells:** Use one-time effect cards for immediate impact, buffs, or debuffs.
3.  **Establish Locations:** Play persistent cards that alter the battlefield or provide ongoing benefits.
4.  **Activate Abilities:** Utilize unique abilities of Characters or Locations.
5.  **Attack & Defend:** Use Characters to attack opponent's Characters or directly reduce their Life Points.

The primary win condition is to reduce the opponent's Life Points to zero.
