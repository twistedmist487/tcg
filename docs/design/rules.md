# Conspiracy TCG - Rules Draft

This document outlines the foundational rules for the Conspiracy TCG.

## 1. Game Components

*   **Deck:** Each player needs a deck of 40 cards.
    *   A deck can contain a maximum of 3 copies of any given card (by name).
    *   A deck must be associated with a single faction (Illuminati, Templars, or Reptilians).
*   **Card Types:**
    *   **Character Cards:** The primary units on the field. They have Attack, Health, and unique abilities.
    *   **Spell Cards:** One-time use cards that provide immediate effects (damage, buffs, debuffs, etc.).
    *   **Location Cards:** Persistent cards that remain on the field, providing ongoing effects or modifying the battlefield.
*   **Faction Energy:** Each faction uses a unique energy type:
    *   **Illuminati:** Influence
    *   **Templars:** Faith
    *   **Reptilians:** Psionics
*   **Life Points:** Each player starts with 30 Life Points.

## 2. Game Setup

1.  Each player shuffles their deck and places it face-down.
2.  Each player draws 4 cards for their starting hand.
3.  The player who goes first is determined randomly (e.g., coin flip).
4.  The player who goes first does not draw a card on their first turn.

## 3. Turn Structure

Each turn consists of the following phases:

### 3.1. Draw Phase

*   The active player draws 1 card from their deck.
*   (Exception: The player who goes first does not draw on their first turn.)

### 3.2. Main Phase

*   **Energy Generation:** The active player gains 1 unit of their faction's energy (Influence, Faith, or Psionics). This energy persists between turns.
*   **Actions:** The active player can perform the following actions in any order, as long as they have sufficient energy and valid targets:
    *   **Play a Character Card:** Pay the card's energy cost to put a Character card from your hand onto the field. Characters enter the field "exhausted" (cannot attack or use abilities on the turn they are played, unless specified otherwise).
    *   **Play a Spell Card:** Pay the card's energy cost to resolve its effect, then discard it.
    *   **Play a Location Card:** Pay the card's energy cost to put a Location card onto the field. Locations remain in play and provide continuous effects. (Limit: 1 Location per player on the field at a time).
    *   **Activate Character/Location Abilities:** Pay the energy cost to use a special ability of a Character or Location you control.
    *   **Declare Attacks:** Choose an active (non-exhausted) Character you control to attack.
        *   **Target:** You may attack an enemy Character or the opponent directly.
        *   **Combat:**
            *   If attacking an enemy Character: The attacking Character deals damage equal to its Attack to the defending Character. The defending Character simultaneously deals damage equal to its Attack to the attacking Character.
            *   If attacking the opponent directly: The attacking Character deals damage equal to its Attack to the opponent's Life Points.
        *   **Exhaustion:** After attacking, a Character becomes "exhausted" and cannot attack again until the next turn.

### 3.3. End Phase

*   Resolve any "end of turn" effects.
*   Become the inactive player. The other player then starts their turn.

## 4. Card Interactions & Keywords

*   **Attack:** The amount of damage a Character deals in combat.
*   **Health:** The amount of damage a Character can sustain before being defeated.
*   **Exhausted:** A state for Characters that have just been played or have attacked. Exhausted Characters cannot attack or use abilities that require them to be "active" until the start of their controller's next turn.
*   **Taunt:** Enemy Characters must attack a Character with Taunt if able.
*   **Stealth:** A Character with Stealth cannot be targeted by enemy attacks or abilities until it attacks or its effect is removed.
*   **Silence:** Removes all abilities from a Character or Location until end of turn/permanently.

## 5. Win Condition

*   A player wins if their opponent's Life Points are reduced to 0.
*   A player loses if they cannot draw a card from their deck (deck out).
